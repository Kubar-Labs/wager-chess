# Business Requirements Document (BRD): Wager Chess

## 1. Executive Summary
Wager Chess is a novel financial gaming application deployed on the Monad testnet. It introduces a hyper-financialized variant of chess where every move has a monetary cost attached to it. The objective is to create an engaging, high-stakes PVP environment where players optimize their financial outlay against their probability of winning, creating a unique intersection of game theory and decentralized finance.

## 2. Business Objectives
- **Demonstrate High-Throughput EVM Capabilities:** Utilize the Monad testnet to process real-time, micro-transactions seamlessly without disrupting the gameplay loop.
- **Pioneer "Pay-Per-Move" Gaming:** Validate the market appetite for micro-transaction-based competitive gaming.
- **User Acquisition & Engagement:** Attract crypto-native gamers and DeFi enthusiasts through a novel optimization puzzle (baiting opponent spending vs. securing the pot).

## 3. Target Audience
- Web3 Gamers & Degens.
- Chess enthusiasts interested in game-theoretic financial stakes.
- The Monad ecosystem community testing new dApps.

## 4. Key Mechanics
- **Micro-transaction Moves:** Each piece type or action incurs a specific cost (e.g., Queen move = $9, Pawn move = $1, Check = $5).
- **Pot Accumulation:** All move costs are aggregated into a central prize pot.
- **Winner-Takes-All:** The player who achieves checkmate (or wins via opponent resignation/timeout) claims the entire pot.
- **Dynamic Optimization:** Players must balance the cost of aggressive moves against the risk of losing their invested capital.

## 5. Success Metrics
- Smart contract deployment and successful execution on Monad Testnet.
- Sub-second transaction finality for moves (leveraging Monad's speed).
- Total Volume Locked (TVL) in game pots.
- Number of active games and daily active users (DAU).
